# Sharing with others

Sharing is opened from the **Share** button above the list or from the sharing button at the bottom
left.

![The sharing window, with the creation of the common archive and joining by invitation](/img/en/condivisione.png){.light-only}
![The sharing window, with the creation of the common archive and joining by invitation](/img/en/condivisione-scuro.png){.dark-only}

## If you are the one creating the shared archive

1. Open sharing and create the common archive: your local copy is uploaded as it is, attachments
   included.
2. **Generate an invitation** for each person, giving it a label (the colleague's name): it tells
   you later which access you are revoking.
3. Send the invitation however you prefer: it is a code that can be pasted, or a link that opens
   ArchiView directly.

As owner you see the list of members and can **revoke** anyone's access at any time: from that
moment their synchronisation stops working, with an explicit message.

::: tip One invitation per person
Generating a different invitation for each collaborator is what makes revocation selective. A
single code passed to everyone can only be revoked for everyone.
:::

## If you receive an invitation

1. Open the link, or paste the code into the welcome screen.
2. Choose the **local folder** in which to keep your copy of the archive.

You now have a complete copy on your computer, usable offline, which aligns itself when the network
is available. No Google account or other registration is needed.

As a member you can receive, send and **leave** the archive; invitations remain with the owner.

## What participants see

Everything in the shared archive: records, transcriptions, models, tags, attachments.

What is personal or tied to your machine is **not** shared: the trash, local snapshots, saved
searches, print settings and the OCR language.

## Working well as a group

- **Divide the work by fonds or by series**, not by field. Two people on the same fonds on different
  days do not get in each other's way; two people on the same record on the same afternoon do.
- **Agree on the models before starting.** Changing a model halfway is possible, but whoever has
  already catalogued fifty documents will have to review them.
- **Agree on conventions**: how names are written, which tags exist, how doubts are marked. A
  [controlled vocabulary](/en/guide/vocabularies) removes half the discussions.
- **Download at the beginning and upload at the end** of every session.

## Leaving, revoking, closing

- A **member** can leave whenever they like: their local copy remains, it simply stops
  synchronising.
- The **owner** can revoke one member at a time.
- Everyone always keeps a complete, readable copy of the archive on their own disk: nobody can leave
  you without your data.
