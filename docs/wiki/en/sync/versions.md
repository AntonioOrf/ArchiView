# Change control and history

Two separate panels, both in the left bar. They answer two different questions.

## Change control: "what did I change?"

It lists changes made locally and **not yet synchronised**. Before uploading you can examine them one
by one and **undo selectively** the ones you do not want to keep.

It is the right place to go when:

- you made a bulk change and want to check it before it becomes final for everyone;
- you cannot remember whether you actually made a correction or only thought about it.

## History: "what was it like before?"

It shows earlier versions of the archive saved in the cloud. From here you can:

- **browse** old versions;
- **open them read-only**, to compare them safely;
- **restore** the archive to an earlier version.

::: warning
Restoring takes the whole archive back, not a single record. Before using it, open the version
read-only and check that it really is the right one.
:::

## Note

Change control and History exist because the archive is connected to a remote one. On a local-only
archive the safety nets are different, and they are there anyway: undo/redo, trash, automatic
snapshots and record history, described in
[Trash, snapshots and history](/en/data/trash-snapshots).
