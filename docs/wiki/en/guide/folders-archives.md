# Folders and archives

There are two levels of organisation, and it is important not to confuse them.

## Folders

Inside an archive you can create **folders and subfolders**, just like in your computer's file
manager: by fonds, by series, by year, by ongoing research. They make browsing comfortable.

You find them in the left panel, under the folder icon. Right-click a folder to create, rename or
delete folders.

## Archives (vaults)

An **archive** is a completely separate container, with its own records, models and tags. You can
have as many as you like.

Use different archives when the materials have nothing to do with each other (two separate research
projects, university work and work for a client). Use folders instead when they belong to the same
corpus.

The switcher lets you move quickly from one archive to another, create new local ones, connect a
remote one or join an archive shared with a working group.

## Which one to choose?

| Situation | Solution |
| --- | --- |
| Same fonds, different series | Folders |
| Two projects that never overlap | Separate archives |
| Material to share with colleagues, the rest not | Separate archives (a whole archive is shared) |
| You want to search everything together | A single archive with folders |

Search and filters work inside the open archive: that is the main reason not to fragment too much.
