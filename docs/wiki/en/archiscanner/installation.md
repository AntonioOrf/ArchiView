# Installing ArchiScanner

ArchiScanner is not on the Play Store: you install it from the APK file published with the
[ArchiView releases](https://github.com/AntonioOrf/ArchiView/releases) (the ones whose name
starts with «ArchiScanner»). It is free, like ArchiView.

::: info What you need
- A phone with **Android 7.0 or later** and a 64-bit (ARM) processor: almost every phone from
  recent years has one.
- **Google Play Services**, which provides the page scanner. Phones without Google services (some
  recent Huawei models, for example) cannot scan.
- Free space for the app and for the photos waiting to be sent: a day in the archive can take up a
  few hundred megabytes before sending.
:::

## Downloading and installing

1. On the phone, open the [ArchiView page](https://archiview.web.app) and tap
   **Scarica l'APK** (Download the APK), or download the `ArchiView-Scanner-….apk` file from the
   latest ArchiScanner release.
2. Open the downloaded file (from the download notification or the **Files** app).
3. The first time, Android warns that for security it does not install apps from that source.
   Tap **Settings**, turn on **Allow from this source** and go back. The permission only applies
   to the app you opened the file with (the browser or **Files**), and you can turn it off
   straight afterwards.
4. Tap **Install**. If Play Protect reports an unrecognised app, it is because it does not come
   from the Play Store: choose to install it anyway, or to have it scanned first.
5. Open ArchiScanner: the [welcome](/en/archiscanner/overview#first-run) starts.

The names of the options vary a little between phone makers, but the steps are these.

## Updating

You don't need to repeat all this by hand. At startup the app checks for a new version and offers
**Update**: it downloads the APK, showing the percentage, and opens Android's installer, where you
just tap **Update**. You
can also check from **Settings → App → Check for updates**.

The new version installs over the old one and keeps everything: pages waiting to be sent,
Documents, settings, account.

::: warning Don't uninstall to update
Uninstalling deletes everything the app holds on the phone, including pages not yet sent and your
Documents. To update, always install over the old version.
:::

## Uninstalling

Before uninstalling, check that **To send** is empty: pages still on the phone have not reached
the computer. Documents are lost unless you turned on the
[copy to Drive](/en/archiscanner/documents#copy-to-drive). Then uninstall the app like any other,
by pressing and holding its icon. The app's access to your Drive is revoked at
[myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
