# The interface

The ArchiView window is divided into areas that always stay in place: the header at the top, the
side column on the left, the records in the centre and the status bar at the bottom.

![The main ArchiView window: header at the top, folders and tags on the left, table of records in the centre and the selected record's panel on the right](/img/en/vista-tabella-scuro.png){.dark-only}

## At the top: the header

From left to right:

- the **panel icons**, which change the content of the side column (table below);
- the **path** of the open folder: click any part of it to go back up;
- **Search the archive**, which opens the command list and search (`Ctrl+K`);
- on the right the global actions: scans arriving from the phone, **sharing**, tutorial, report a
  problem, **settings**.

The panel icons:

| Icon | Panel | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Folder | **Folders** | Browsing the archive and its subfolders. This is the normal view |
| Magnifier | **Search** | Searching metadata, titles, transcriptions and OCR text |
| Bookmark | **Tags** | Every label in use; one click filters. Tag management opens from here |
| Changes | **Change control** | What you have changed and not yet sent to the remote archive |
| Clock | **History** | Local snapshots and earlier versions saved in the cloud |

## On the left: archive, folders, saved searches, tags

At the top of the column sits the **archive switcher** (to move between archives, create or
connect them; "Manage archives…" also lists the recent ones). Below it, three sections:
**Folders**, **Saved searches** and **Tags**, with each tag's colour and its number of records.
The first twelve tags are always in view, the rest under "All tags". Right-click a tag to change
its colour.

At the top of the folder tree a row carries the **name of the archive**: it is the root, and it
holds the records that are not yet filed in a folder.

In the tree every record carries its shelfmark; the button at the top right of the **Folders**
panel adds a field of your choice underneath (the declarant, the notary, the author) and lets
you sort the tree by that field. See
[What a record shows in the tree](/en/guide/folders-archives#what-a-record-shows-in-the-tree).

## At the bottom: the status bar

On the left, the **synchronisation status**: if the archive is connected to a remote one, a click
opens the download and upload commands, and the action of the moment (for example "Send") appears
next to it. On a purely local archive there is nothing to watch. On the right, "Saved at…" and the
record counts.

## In the centre: the list of records

Above the list, from left to right:

- **New record** (the chevron preselects the type) and **New model**;
- **Filters** (with a counter of the active filters), **sorting**, and the **grid / table**
  selector;
- the **"⋯"** menu, which gathers the rest: new archive, import and export, visible columns in
  table view, trash, delete archive.

The default view is the **table**: the shelfmark in a monospaced typeface, the main fields and a
**Transcription** column with a dot (transcribed, n of m folios, has text, to do). If you had
already chosen the grid, you keep it.

When a single record is selected, the **record panel** opens on the right: fields, dates read in
normalised form, attachments, links, tags and the **Edit** and **Transcribe** buttons. On a narrow
screen the panel is hidden. Each record's "⋯" opens the same menu as the right mouse button.

Below the list sits the **result counter**; when a selection is active, it also shows how many
records you have selected.

## Three things that save time

- `Ctrl+K` opens the command list: any feature, without hunting through menus.
- `?` shows every keyboard shortcut.
- The **right mouse button** works everywhere: on a record, on a folder, on an archive. Related items
  are grouped in submenus (**View**, **Export**, **On N records**), opened with the mouse or the right
  arrow and closed with the left arrow or `Esc`.

See [Commands and shortcuts](/en/guide/shortcuts).

## The built-in tutorial

The **?** icon in the header restarts the guided tour that runs on first launch at any time.
