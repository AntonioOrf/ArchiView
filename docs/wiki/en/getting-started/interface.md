# The interface

The ArchiView window is divided into three areas that always stay in place.

![The main ArchiView window: panel bar and folder tree on the left, list of records in the centre](/img/en/vista-griglia.png){.light-only}
![The main ArchiView window: panel bar and folder tree on the left, list of records in the centre](/img/en/vista-griglia-scuro.png){.dark-only}

*The three areas: panels and folders on the left, commands at the top, records in the centre.*

## On the left: the panel bar

A column of icons that changes the content of the side panel:

| Icon | Panel | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Folder | **Folders** | Browsing the archive and its subfolders. This is the normal view |
| Magnifier | **Search** | Searching metadata, titles, transcriptions and OCR text |
| Bookmark | **Tags** | Every label in use; one click filters. Tag management opens from here |
| Changes | **Change control** | What you have changed and not yet sent to the remote archive |
| Clock | **History** | Local snapshots and earlier versions saved in the cloud |

At the top of the folder tree a row carries the **name of the archive**: it is the root, and it
holds the records that are not yet filed in a folder.

At the bottom left: the **archive switcher** (to move between archives, create or connect them)
and the **sharing** button.

## At the top: synchronisation status

If the archive is connected to a remote one, a single line summarises the situation and opens the
download and upload commands. On a purely local archive there is nothing to watch.

## In the centre: the list of records

This is where the work happens. Above the list, from left to right:

- **New record** (the chevron preselects the type) and **New model**;
- **Filters** (with a counter of the active filters), **sorting**, and the **grid / table**
  selector;
- the **"⋯"** menu, which gathers the rest: new archive, import and export, visible columns in
  table view, trash, delete archive.

Every record has two direct buttons, **Edit** and **Transcribe**, and a "⋯" that opens the same
menu as the right mouse button.

Below the list sits the **result counter**; when a selection is active, it also shows how many
records you have selected.

## Three things that save time

- `Ctrl+K` opens the command list: any feature, without hunting through menus.
- `?` shows every keyboard shortcut.
- The **right mouse button** works everywhere: on a record, on a folder, on an archive.

See [Commands and shortcuts](/en/guide/shortcuts).

## The built-in tutorial

The **?** icon restarts the guided tour that runs on first launch at any time.
