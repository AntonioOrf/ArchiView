## ArchiView 4.0.3 — Safer scans from the phone

### Fixes

- **Batches stuck on Drive**: a phone upload interrupted by the network could leave a duplicated batch on Drive that the computer refused every time. The batch now arrives and the duplicate copies are moved to the trash.
- **Notes from the phone**: the note of a batch that arrives while the record is open in the form, or in the detached window, is no longer lost when the record is saved.
- **Interrupted assignment**: if saving fails while a batch is being assigned to a record, the record goes back to how it was and the assignment can be repeated, without the "file exists" error and without duplicate pages.
