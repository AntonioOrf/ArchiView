## ArchiView 4.0.5 — Scan notes

### Fixes

- **Sheet notes from the phone**: a note written on a page in ArchiScanner now reaches that sheet. It shows in a box over the image in the transcription view, can be edited or hidden, and appears as a caption in the documents gallery.
- **Record note visible**: the note sent from the phone now shows in the form even for types without a Notes field, such as Notarial imbreviature, including records imported earlier.

## ArchiView 4.0.4 — Archives created on the phone

### New

- **Archives created with ArchiScanner**: a new archive created on the phone shows up on Drive right away and opens on the computer from "Join a Shared Archive" → "Browse Google Drive…". If the folder is yours, the archive becomes a Personal backup instead of a Shared archive.
