## ArchiView 4.0.0 — A new interface

The whole program has a new look: paper and ink, typefaces made for reading, fewer boxes and more room for the document. Your data, archives and sync stay exactly as they were.

### New

- **New typefaces, bundled with the program**: IBM Plex Sans for controls, Source Serif 4 for record values and titles, IBM Plex Mono for shelfmarks, sheets and numbers. They work offline too.
- **Four redesigned themes** with the paper-and-ink palette, light and dark.
- **New header**: clickable folder path, a search box that opens the palette (Ctrl+K), global actions on the right. The sidebar has the archive switcher at the top and Folders, Saved searches and Tags sections; at the bottom a status bar shows cloud state, last save and counts.
- **Table view by default**, with a Transcription dot column (transcribed, n of m, has text, to do) and a right-hand panel for the selected record. If you were using the grid, you keep it.
- **Record form in sections** (Identification, Description, Attachments, Tags and links) with an index on the right, a title that follows the shelfmark and a Cancel / Save record bar (Ctrl+S) always at the bottom.
- **Transcription with the source on the left**, line numbers, a Leiden toolbar for gaps, expansions and *sic*, and a Source / Side by side / Text switch. At the bottom: line, total and word count.
- **Two-pane document type editor**, with a preview of how the field looks in the record and how many records use the type.
- **Two-column Settings** and a theme picker with previews; Manage archives now lists recent archives.
- **Sync conflicts** resolved field by field, with the two versions side by side to click.
- **Tag colour from the right-click menu**, in the sidebar, on records and in the form.
- Dialogs, history, ZIP/IIIF/CSV import, OCR, print, recycle bin, vocabularies, people and places, graph, collation and the tour all follow the new style.

### Fixes

- **Drive revisions that cannot be downloaded** (uploaded by another member, or with downloads restricted): a message now explains why, instead of a generic error.
- Dropdown items are readable in dark themes; the X on the welcome screen is large enough to click.
- Progress bars and panels no longer stutter while animating.
