## ArchiView 3.4.0 — The record next to the document

Fill in the record while you look at the document, in the same window or in a separate one.

### New

- **"Record" tab in the transcription**: next to "Text" there is now "Record", with the full form. It saves by itself after a short pause ("Saved at 10:42"), and Ctrl+Shift+S switches between the two tabs. The text you are transcribing is never overwritten by the form.
- **"Document" in the record form**: while editing a record with attachments, "Document" shows the page on the right, with zoom, page and folio navigation, and "Image | Transcription" to read the transcribed text (read only, with a link to the editor).
- **Separate window**: the record (or the document) can be opened in its own window, handy with two monitors. "Dock back", closing the window or dragging it onto the left column ("Release to dock") puts it back in place. Both windows work on the same record and nothing is lost when switching.
- **Resizable columns**: drag the divider, use the ← → arrows, or double-click to go back to half. It snaps to 1/3, 1/2 and 2/3, and the proportion is remembered separately for "Text" and "Record".
- **ZIP import with progress**: importing a ZIP archive shows file N of M, then how many records, folders and attachments came in; errors (not enough disk space, invalid archive) are explained, with "Try again".

### Fixes

- Saving a record no longer risks overwriting transcriptions, sheet status or regions written in the meantime: the form's attachments are merged with the current ones, as in sync.
