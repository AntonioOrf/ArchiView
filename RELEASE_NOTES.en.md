## ArchiView 3.3.1 — Notes and "to review" boxes with ArchiScanner 0.3.0

Goes with ArchiScanner 0.3.0, the new version of the Android app.

### New

- **Notes from the phone in the record**: a note written on the phone for a page ("trimmed", "water stain") lands in the record's **Notes** field, one line per page with the folio label (`1v: trimmed`), also when the pages are merged into a PDF. The batch note is now added to an existing record too, instead of being lost.
- **Boxes to review**: records tagged `da vedere` (or `da consultare`) show up marked on the phone, which filters them with "To review" to plan a day in the archive. The tags stay on the computer: only the mark reaches the phone.
